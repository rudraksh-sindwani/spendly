Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Spendly is a personal expense tracker, so almost everything it holds is information you typed in yourself. This policy explains what we collect, why we hold it, and what we do not do with it.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what the service needs in order to work:

  • Account details — the username or email address you register with, and a securely hashed version of your password. We never store your password in readable form.
  • Expense records — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic technical data — a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and standard server logs such as request times and error details.

We do not ask for bank account numbers, card numbers, or other payment credentials, and you should not enter them into any field. Spendly only needs the amount you spent, not the instrument you paid with.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to run the service and nothing else. Specifically, to:

  • Show your expense history, category breakdowns, and monthly summaries.
  • Sign you in and keep your session active.
  • Diagnose errors and keep the application working correctly.

We do not sell your data, rent it, or share it with advertisers. We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not use your expense records to make decisions about you.

3. Data Storage

Your records are stored in the application’s database and kept for as long as your account is active. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, so they cannot be read back by us or by anyone who obtains the stored data.

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We recommend keeping your own copy of any records that matter to you.

You can delete individual expenses at any time from within the application. If you delete your account, the associated expense records are removed with it; residual copies may persist briefly in routine backups before being overwritten.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly keeps outside dependencies to a minimum. The application loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google’s servers and they may see your IP address as part of that request. No expense data is ever sent to them.

If the application is hosted by a third-party provider, that provider necessarily stores the underlying database and server logs on our behalf. We do not pass your data to any other third party except where we are legally required to do so.

5. Your Choices

You can view and correct your expense records at any time by editing them in the application, and you can stop using Spendly and delete your account whenever you wish. If you would like a copy of your data or want it erased, get in touch using the details below.

6. Contact Us

If you have a question about this policy or about how your data is handled, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.

We may update this policy as the service develops. When we do, we will revise the “last updated” date at the top of this page.
